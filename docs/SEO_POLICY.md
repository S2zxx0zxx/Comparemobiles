# CompareMobile SEO data policy

SEO output must reflect the same source-backed catalog shown to users.

- Product JSON-LD may include name, brand, market, status, specifications, canonical URL and official source references.
- Do not emit price Offers until the retailer offer is source-backed, region-matched and fresh enough for the live-price rule.
- Do not emit AggregateRating or Review unless CompareMobile owns a valid review/rating system with documented methodology.
- Do not generate thin filter permutations for indexing.
- Budget pages are blocked until verified pricing coverage is sufficient.
- Region-specific records must remain region-specific in metadata and structured data.
